How the bot picks
Projection. For every line it pulls the player's last 5 seasons and weights the last 5 games, last 10, this season and last season. Then it adjusts for: how the opponent defends that stat vs the player's position; the player's home/road split; NBA back-to-backs (measured league-wide from the data); the game total and spread (high totals = more stats, big NBA spreads = blowout risk for starters, NFL favorites run more and trailing teams throw more); and injuries — when a teammate is out it checks how this player did in past games without him.

Probability. It blends a model probability with how often the player cleared the line, pulls small samples toward 50%, and trims overconfidence (backtested on last season). If you add a sportsbook line, the books get 60% of the vote. Once you have 30+ graded picks it re-tunes itself to your actual results.

Lineups. It tests every combo of your top 14 picks from 2 to 6, Power and Flex, with PrizePicks' rules, and accounts for picks in the same game moving together (a QB and his WR both going over, for example).

Grades compare to the 2-pick break-even: A 5+ points above, B 2+, C barely, Pass below. Stats, schedule and injuries refresh every morning. Real edges are small — only bet what you're fine losing. Help: 1-800-GAMBLER.
